AOCI Runtime Contract (session level)

AOCI gives models stable, versioned, incrementally maintainable repository-level cognition.

Authority boundary: this contract sets session principles only. The current Guide carries the execution order and safety stops for the current Plan; Schema, Spec, and Validator carry machine structures and criteria. Prompt governs model semantic generation only. Descriptions, Help, AGENTS, and docs cannot override machine facts. Machine numbers, vocabularies, and sets come only from internal/machinecontract in the compiled version. In a Fresh Bootstrap the Host model authors the semantic artifacts required by the current Plan and Guide; AOCI never sets origin=host_model, manufactures a receipt, or generates semantics. Internal batches are not user decisions; approval, safety, drift, CAS, and Recovery boundaries still hold.

1. At the start of an Agent Run read aoci_rules once, then request aoci_overview. While continuation_required=true, submit the exact next_cursor until completed=true, without asking the user or starting the task. Stop on Host truncation, a missing, duplicate, or reordered Chunk, a cursor error, or an Index or chunk_tokens change; after truncation ask the user to lower overview_delivery.chunk_tokens and restart. Until Attestation completes, never supplement Whole-Index cognition from Memory, source, Spec, aoci.txt, history, scope, search, or Entry reads. Then confirm delivery and submit one model-cognition-attestation/v1 echoing the Challenge's exact index_sha256, entry_sequence_sha256, and entry_count; an ordinal is the 1-based position among formal Entries only. One same-response schema error may be corrected without changing answers; an object, Tag, or F mismatch means model_attestation=fail and uncertain assimilation, with no retry or bypass: it disables an unqualified complete-system claim and, during initial cognition, unbound Root/Meta, Migration, and system decisions. The result retains delivered cognition's reported scope and state. system_mastery_percent self-assesses only the system framework, apart from Index coverage. On success say only: “Fully read and verified AOCI: <entry_count>/<entry_count> Entries, about <estimated_tokens> tokens, <chunk_count> Chunks, Challenge <passed>/<total>, Index coverage <coverage_percent>%; my system-framework mastery is about <system_mastery_percent>%.” On a delivery fault say only: “AOCI complete read failed: received <received>/<total> Chunks, Index coverage about <coverage>% because <reason>; complete system-framework cognition cannot currently be claimed.” A complete response yields a receipt bound to runtime_repository_root, index_sha256, mcp_service_version, and cognition_scope; Memory cannot replace it.

1a. The levels no_cognition, index_loaded, delivery_verified, cognition_verified, and cognition_governed arrive with their own message; delivery_verified means loaded and delivery-verified, never "no cognition".

1b. cognition_state_version=cognition-state/v2 adds independent delivery_verified, model_cognition_usable, strict_attestation_verified, governance_aligned, and current_system_cognition_reliable; a strict mismatch alone does not make model cognition unusable. Only current_system_cognition_reliable=true permits an unqualified current complete-system cognition claim. valid, uncertain, and invalid keep their own dimension.

2. Reuse a reliable complete Overview instead of re-reading it. Checkpoint reasons: context_compaction (after Host compaction), semantic_threshold (machine-counted semantic changes reached cognition_refresh_threshold), and phase_transition (a major phase boundary such as implementation to system testing, not a function, test, or tool call). They advise and never deny an explicit request. Declare context_compaction or phase_transition through aoci_overview with a refresh_event_id; semantic_threshold is machine-derived, and simultaneous reasons merge.

2a. After a known Host compaction prior cognition is unreliable and the handoff carries no Index semantics. Before continuing, re-read aoci_rules if it is not reliably present, declare context_compaction with a fresh refresh_event_id, and make an ordinary complete aoci_overview request, not check_only or a probe: follow every next_cursor, confirm delivery, attest once. delivery_mode=capacity_stop with host_context_capacity_exceeded means the Whole-Index does not fit this Host: do not retry or lower chunk_tokens; continue source-bound without an unqualified complete-system claim. A probe is valid only when no compaction is known.

2b. With semantic changes or blockers, let managed objects stabilize, then maintain and prove alignment as in 7. Results are refresh_not_required, refresh_required, refresh_deferred_until_stable, or refresh_ready_for_overview, bodiless only under check_only=true. Every ordinary aoci_overview delivers the complete requested scope from a coherent snapshot, receipt or not; the one exception is that capacity stop. A complete, host-confirmed, aligned Attestation attempt consumes the merged reasons and advances the generation: a pass records a reliable receipt, a partial or failed one an uncertain receipt with delivery_guidance=full_system_claim_disabled_source_bound_task_continuation_allowed, and neither loops Overview. Dirty or Stale cognition keeps its state; pending Recovery or an incoherent snapshot fails closed.

3. To establish or complete the index, use the current AOCI Guide and only the commands and safety stops it returns; aoci_maintain is for incremental maintenance of an established index.

4. The model authors cognition: the Header from investigated repository facts, an Entry after reading the current Header, the target source, tests, configuration, and necessary related evidence. Paths, an AST, symbol lists, scans, regular expressions, templates, or rule engines may locate, validate, and write facts, never generate, prefill, assemble, or rewrite tags, F/R/A/S, or Curation decisions.

5. AOCI-managed write boundaries: when the user limits only business files, closing may update aoci.txt and Baseline; when the user forbids changes to aoci.txt, .aoci, metadata, or any additional file, write nothing and report the remaining inconsistency.

6. Read-only tasks need no maintenance call. The running version is cognition_receipt.mcp_service_version; the binary is the .mcp.json command, not necessarily on PATH.

7. When managed objects change, call aoci_maintain once after they reach the task's final stable state. After aligned=true and status=applied with refresh_ready_for_overview, do not maintain or write again; finish Verify, Check, and Guide, then judge whether the next phase needs a complete Overview. format-only drift needs no new semantics.

8. For actual candidates use the current reliable Header's dictionaries, calling aoci_header if none is in context; read every affected object and its evidence; preserve every source_sha256, candidate_id, and batch identity; submit the complete batch once through aoci_update_entry entries. max_entries limits one request; while remaining is nonzero call Maintain again. Never slice a batch or reduce Managed Scope for transport.

9. For repair_required with findings, repair only the named candidates and resubmit the same complete batch. stopped ends that attempt: inspect failed_step, error, and recovery; replan a proven zero-write attempt, resume a provable Intent, or finish a policy-selected Rollback; stop the task only when proof is missing, third-party bytes conflict, replanning does not converge, approval or external action is needed, or another safety boundary applies. Never bypass conflicts, approvals, permissions, human decisions, CAS, or safety signals.

10. With insufficient evidence for a semantic decision, do not guess. Use aoci_report only when the current layout and returned tool state support it; it does not replace Curation, orphan decisions, or recovery. A managed change after maintenance invalidates its result.

11. Specialized workflows follow only the current Guide, tool descriptions, Prompt, and CLI Help. Wrong cognition is more harmful than temporary absence.
