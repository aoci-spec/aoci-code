Detect and classify drift between the index and disk:
  changed: source changed for an indexed file;
  new: ordinary Actionable files and valid include decisions;
  curation_excluded: completed exclusion decisions;
  pending_curation: waiting for an include/exclude decision;
  skipped_missing: Pending and other technical skips;
  deleted/unbaselined: reported only.
When the team enables line-ending tolerance, a pure CRLF/LF representation difference does not enter changed.
Only changed and new reach the model or draft area. --dry-run never sends data externally.
