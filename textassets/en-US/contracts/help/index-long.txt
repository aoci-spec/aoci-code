aoci index groups the index bootstrap, generation, maintenance, and quality-governance workflows.
inventory:      list files present on disk but absent from the index, with profiles; fully offline.
header draft:   call the user-configured endpoint to draft a Header; writes only to the draft area.
build:          draft initial Entries in batches; writes only to the draft area.
update:         detect drift and govern it according to automation.mode.
entries:        Check, Diff, and atomic Apply.
score:          score index quality across nine dimensions.
agent:          Plan, Guide, and Stage protocol for a host Agent.
