AOCI Managed Scope v2 and Cognition Budget v1

Status: stable machine contract. Executable identifiers and enumerations are
owned by internal/machinecontract. This Spec explains their semantics and must
not become a copied validator or fallback authority.

1. One Whole-Index

AOCI retains one logical complete repository cognition index. Managed Scope is
source-governance policy, not a second index, query replacement, or permission
to present a partial body as complete cognition. Legacy and Volumes remain
compatible physical layouts. Source, tests, configuration, and database
structure remain the highest implementation evidence.

2. Roles

index requires one model-authored Entry, participates in the Whole-Index and
the cognition Baseline, and produces Missing, Orphan, Stale, and Unbaselined.
observe has no Entry and consumes no Whole-Index tokens, but its deterministic
source fingerprint remains in the same Baseline. New, changed, and removed
observe paths produce ObservedNew, ObservedChanged, and ObservedRemoved. Under
review_required, an exact reviewed set must advance through the governed Scope
transaction. exclude has no Entry or fingerprint; its content is not opened and
does not enter Prompt, Evidence, Candidate, or Ledger bodies.

3. Policy and evaluation

managed-scope-policy/v2 contains profile, observe_change_policy,
approval_mode (inherit, auto, or review), and ordered
rules. Rules contain rule_id, action, pattern, pattern_kind, reason, optional
structured decision_basis, source, created_by, order, enabled, and optional
exceptions. decision_basis distinguishes semantic_density,
test_responsibility_delegation, generated_asset, locale_delegation,
public_private_boundary, duplicate_semantics, maintainer_direction, and the
forbidden reduction basis transport_constraint without parsing free-form
reason text. The policy also owns
count and percentage thresholds that remain risk facts for compatibility;
reduction size alone is not an Auto approval gate. Paths are repository
relative, slash-normalized, non-escaping, and deterministic. File, directory,
and glob rules can match future paths. Stable last-match ordering applies within
one source priority. The precedence is Safe Inventory hard denial, approved
high-risk exact opt-in, user, exact Curation, profile, Git facts, then default.
Every evaluation explains its winning rule, source, priority, safety state,
content-read state, Whole-Index participation, fingerprint participation, and
reason.

production indexes ordinary Safe Inventory candidates, observes test source,
and excludes testdata, fixtures, Golden bodies, and snapshots. full indexes
tests too. custom requires explicit project rules. User rules can override
non-safety profile behavior.

4. Safety

Safe Inventory v2 runs before policy evaluation. Devices, unsafe links or
reparse boundaries, AOCI runtime transactions, database runtime data, VCS
objects, generated/runtime directories, secrets, sockets, PID/log files, and
other hard-denied objects cannot be reopened by a normal rule. A sensitive
object can be considered only by an exact-file opt-in and a real digest-bound
human TTY approval before content read. Directory opt-ins and opt-ins for
devices, boundary escapes, database runtime, or AOCI transaction assets are
invalid. Secret templates such as .env.example are not secrets by name alone.

5. Direct edits and transitions

Configuration is desired policy. The active policy and budget identities, plus
the exact applied budget policy needed to prove tightening versus relaxation,
live in the existing Baseline receipt. A direct edit never changes Entries or
fingerprints; Verify, Check, Status, Maintain, and Guide report
scope_change_required until one governed Apply. index-to-observe and
index-to-exclude require one scope-entry-disposition/v1 per removed Entry.
Only the model decides unique semantics and authors transfer candidates.
Programs validate binding and complete coverage; they never decide semantic
value. Legacy observe/exclude-to-index requires a source-bound complete Entry
candidate. Volumes may first activate an Entry-less source as Index through the
governed Scope transaction; that explicit authoring debt remains Missing and
must then be completed through machine-issued Maintain batches before alignment.

6. Apply authorization

aoci-apply-authorization-policy/v1 is the executable authorization authority.
approval_mode=inherit follows the team-owned automation.mode. An explicit auto
or review is a project override, except automation.mode=off always wins and
forbids Apply. Effective auto uses standing team authorization; review requires
interactive_digest_confirmation in a real character-device TTY; legacy keeps
the prior compatibility boundary; off permits Plan and Preview but writes
nothing.

Auto is not model self-approval and does not omit evidence. After deterministic
replay and all hard gates, AOCI creates aoci-policy-bound-approval/v1 before
Intent. The immutable Receipt binds operation, repository identity, automation
and Scope modes, authorization-policy identity, Envelope and Preview digests,
current Index/Baseline/config/Scope/Curation identities, projected
Index/Baseline and Whole-Index cost, Entry and role counts, complete Retention
Review, P0/P1, write and guard sets, recovery direction, audit time, and its own
approval digest. Apply validates the Receipt again under the global lock and
stores it in the recovery Intent. A Receipt cannot authorize another Envelope;
policy or formal-preimage drift invalidates it. Resume reuses the immutable
Intent Receipt and never asks for a new approval. Repeated Apply is idempotent.

Auto permits governed Entry create, update, and retirement; every Scope role
transition; reviewed Curation and semantic-transfer candidates; observe
fingerprint advancement; and proven budget tightening. It requires complete
model-owned Retention disposition and reviewed transfers, P0=P1=0, no R/S or
projected maximum violation, no pending transaction or third-party bytes,
deterministic replay, exact CAS guards, and available recovery. A large Entry
count is not independently blocking.

An Index-to-Observe/Exclude transition for a Missing, Stale, or Unbaselined
authoring target is a cognition_coverage_reduction. It is recorded with path,
old/new role, authoring state, rule identity, and structured decision basis,
raises P1, and cannot receive policy_bound_auto authorization. A path the
current Safe Inventory no longer sees has neither an Entry nor source bytes left
to lose, so retiring its stale Baseline record is bookkeeping rather than a
reduction; excluded paths remain in the evaluation, so only a deleted source
reports that state. A transport, batch, payload, token, or single-request limit
is never an admissible automatic coverage-reduction basis;
decision_basis=transport_constraint adds the explicit
transport_constraint_not_allowed blocker. Independent Scope review may still
authorize normal evidence-backed density changes. Existing complete Entry
retirement retains its prior disposition and authorization contract.

Lowering the applied authorization posture is an approval_policy_relaxation.
It cannot receive policy_bound_auto authorization, because a posture may never
ratify its own weakening, and the weaker desired posture never decides that no
reviewer is needed: the transition is authorized under the posture the current
Baseline receipt proves, so exactly one independent Scope review can ratify it.
A posture is therefore tightened and relaxed through the same governed path.

Auto fails with blocked facts rather than falling back to a confirmation phrase
for sensitive-content inclusion, source-file writes, unsafe filesystem
boundaries, Root/Meta/Volume lifecycle, budget relaxation, enforce-to-observe,
weakened safety, explicit or incomplete semantic drops, P0/P1, third-party
bytes, incomplete batches, or unavailable recovery. Automatic workflows never
silently delete source files or ungoverned Entries. A governed role transition
may atomically retire formal Entries without deleting business source.

7. One transaction

managed-scope-change-plan/v2 freezes old/new policy and budget identities,
every role and Entry delta, Retention Review, Baseline and observe deltas,
before/after token reports, risk, write/guard sets, and recovery direction.
Risk distinguishes any approval-bound budget_policy_change from a proven
budget_relaxation; Legacy observe to a stricter enforce policy is not relaxation.
Preview and the mode-selected authorization Receipt bind one Envelope digest.
Apply reuses the global cognition
lock, CAS, AtomicWrite, Baseline, Ledger, receipt/archive, and recovery pipeline.
The Preview is also a managed-scope-change-envelope/v2 document through its
explicit envelope_version and digest. Desired configuration is a CAS guard;
the Baseline receipt is the active policy identity. Filesystem case
sensitivity participates in that identity, and a receipt established under
the opposite case semantics is accepted only when the machine proves, path by
path, that both semantics assign identical roles and fingerprint
participation; any real divergence keeps scope_change_required. The envelope source guard
binds the live managed-source snapshot at Plan time, separately from the
projected Baseline; Apply accepts only its own bound Root postimage and rejects
any other post-Plan source drift. Apply advances aoci.txt,
an optional reviewed Curation postimage, Baseline roles/fingerprints, and audit
state as one formal transaction. Baseline is last. A
partial transaction is resumed or rolled back from immutable pre/postimages;
repeated Apply is idempotent. Third-party changes fail closed.

8. Budget

cognition-budget-policy/v1 has mode observe or enforce, Whole-Index target,
warning and maximum tokens, and complete non-overlapping C1-C9 target/maximum
bands for R and S. Old projects without materialized policy use observe;
new-project init materializes enforce. Project values are configurable and are
not global constants.

Reports measure Header, F, R, A, S, structure, total, largest Entries, largest
R/S, and field violations. Only formal index-role cognition bytes count toward
Whole-Index. A candidate is never truncated, summarized, retagged, or assigned
a lower C by the program. In enforce mode an ordinary Entry Apply validates the
candidate R/S and the final projected Whole-Index before any write. The unified
Scope transaction applies the same hard field and projected maximum gates even
during a migration from observe mode. Failure is repair_required with current,
projected, target, warning, maximum, delta, largest objects, and suggested
model-owned compression targets.

Maintain candidates carry the complete R/S band policy, exact limits for an
existing Entry's C level, current Whole-Index cost, remaining headroom, Scope
role, and observe-evidence state. Before model output exists, projected cost is
the current baseline and projection_pending is true; the submitted candidate
receives the authoritative projected gate.

9. Compatibility and delivery

Baseline fingerprints without role mean index. Legacy config and Baseline are
readable and remain observe-budget compatible. The MCP surface stays at nine
tools. Volumes, Migration/Reversal, Database Evidence/Binding, Auto Onboarding,
dual Locale, no-network core, and CGO-free builds retain their contracts.
Ordinary Overview continues to deliver the complete formal Whole-Index; Scope,
search, or chunks cannot substitute for it.
